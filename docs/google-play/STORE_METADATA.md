# FlowOS — Google Play Store metadata

## App name
FlowOS

## Short description (80 characters max)
Organize tasks, events and reminders around what you commit to do.

## Full description

FlowOS brings your tasks, events and reminders together so you can turn what you need to do into a clear, manageable plan.

Capture commitments quickly, review your day at a glance, and keep your schedule organized across FlowOS and the Google services you choose to connect.

### Plan your day
- See today's tasks and events in one place.
- Organize tasks by priority and execution order.
- Set start times, deadlines, duration and all-day activities.
- Manage recurring activities and custom reminders.

### Stay in sync
- Synchronize supported Google Calendar events and Google Tasks when you connect your Google account.
- Edit activities in FlowOS and send changes to Google.
- Use Android widgets to see today's activities and your calendar at a glance.
- Receive local reminders and a daily summary when notifications are enabled.

### Keep control
- Use voice commands to add, edit, move, complete or delete activities.
- Review and manage your commitments in Today, Calendar and List views.
- Disconnect Google from settings when you no longer want to sync.
- Request deletion of your FlowOS account and associated data from within the app or through the public deletion page.

FlowOS helps you organize your commitments; it does not make changes to your schedule without your action. Google synchronization requires you to connect and authorize your Google account. Features that depend on notifications require the relevant device permissions.
