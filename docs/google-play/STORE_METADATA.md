## Short description (80 characters max)
Organize tasks, events and reminders around what you commit to do.

## Full description
FlowOS brings tasks, calendar events and reminders together in one simple planning system.

Capture what you need to do, turn it into clear commitments, plan your day and keep your schedule under control.

### What FlowOS helps you do
- Capture tasks and commitments quickly.
- See today's events and tasks in one place.
- Plan work around deadlines, duration and available time.
- Manage reminders and recurring activities.
- Synchronize supported Google Calendar and Google Tasks data when you choose to connect Google.
- Get an optional daily weather summary based on your approximate location.
- Use optional AI-powered planning suggestions to help prioritize and organize open commitments.
- Keep control of your data with an in-app account deletion flow.

FlowOS is designed to help you make better planning decisions. It does not automatically change your schedule on your behalf.
