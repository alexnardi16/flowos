Delete your FlowOS account

This page lets you request deletion of your FlowOS account and associated FlowOS data without reinstalling the app.

Request account deletion

Enter the Google email address used for your FlowOS account. Do not enter your Google password.

What will be deleted

  • Your FlowOS authentication account.
  • Tasks, events, reminders and other FlowOS commitments associated with the account.
  • Google connection information and OAuth tokens stored by FlowOS.
  • Other FlowOS account and application data associated with the account.

How requests are processed

After receiving a request, we may contact you to verify that the request belongs to the account owner. We then delete the account and associated FlowOS data without undue delay, subject to any information that must be retained under applicable law. Deletion is irreversible.

If you prefer, you may also contact the privacy address directly: alex16nardi@gmail.com.
