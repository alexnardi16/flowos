FlowOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

App and privacy contact

App: FlowOS. For privacy questions or requests, contact support.flowos@gmail.com.

1. Information FlowOS processes

FlowOS processes information needed to provide the service: your FlowOS account identifier; Google sign-in profile information (name, email and Google account identifier); tasks, events, reminders and other commitments you create (including titles, descriptions, dates, deadlines, duration, context, priority and status); Google Calendar events and Google Tasks that you choose to synchronize; selected calendar/task-list identifiers and synchronization metadata; and, when notifications are enabled, your device's Expo push token.

App preferences, AI settings (provider, model and enabled/disabled state), and widget appearance settings are stored locally on your device. Your provider API key is stored in the operating system's secure storage on the device. It is not uploaded to FlowOS or synchronized with your FlowOS account or Google. You can remove it in Settings → FlowOS AI → Delete API key. Removing your FlowOS account does not revoke or delete an API key held with an external AI provider.

2. Google sign-in and synchronization

Google sign-in provides basic profile information. If you connect Google Calendar or Google Tasks, FlowOS accesses only the data covered by the permissions you authorize and the calendars/task lists you select. Google OAuth access and refresh tokens, account identifiers, selected destinations and sync metadata are stored by the FlowOS backend in a private database schema and are not exposed to the app client. Google data is used to provide the synchronization you request, not for advertising or sale.

3. Optional FlowOS AI features and data sent to AI providers

FlowOS AI is optional and is OFF by default. When it is OFF, AI features are unavailable and FlowOS continues to provide its non-AI functionality; FlowOS does not send user content or activity data to an AI provider for these features. A fixed, non-personal test prompt is sent only if you explicitly select Test connection. To use AI, you must enable it, select a provider, enter your own API key and affirmatively confirm each AI operation before the relevant content is sent.

AI requests are sent directly from your device to the provider you selected, using the API key you supplied. FlowOS does not proxy AI prompts through its Supabase backend and does not store AI prompts or generated responses in your FlowOS account. The selected provider receives and processes the request under its own terms, privacy policy, retention settings and data-use practices. Providers may change those practices. Some free services may use submitted content to improve their services or models. Review the provider's current terms and privacy controls before use.

  • Voice command interpretation: if you accept the in-app confirmation, the recognized voice transcript and titles of active FlowOS activities are sent to the selected AI provider to interpret the command. Activity descriptions and notes are not included. Audio recognition itself is handled by the system/browser speech-recognition service and may be subject to that service's terms.
  • Birthdays and anniversaries: if you confirm, the selected occasion's title/person name and date, plus the selected message channel, tone and language, are sent to the provider. Notes and descriptions are not sent. FlowOS does not send the message automatically.
  • Web research and product comparisons: the request you type and any source excerpts, URLs, product details or notes you paste are sent to the selected provider. Integrated Google Search grounding is currently available only when Google Gemini is selected; Google may process the search query and related request data under its own terms.
  • Professional messages: the recipient or role, purpose, context you enter, selected tone, channel and language are sent to the selected provider. Review the generated text before copying or opening another app. FlowOS does not send the message automatically.
  • Connection test: when you explicitly select Test connection, FlowOS sends a fixed, non-personal test prompt to the selected provider.

Supported provider choices include OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, Mistral, Groq, xAI, OpenRouter and a custom OpenAI-compatible HTTPS endpoint. If you configure a custom endpoint, that endpoint operator receives the request and API key. The API key is transmitted to the provider as an authentication credential; it is not stored on FlowOS servers.

Important: Do not send confidential work information, employer or customer data, passwords, authentication tokens, sensitive personal information, or information about other people unless you have a lawful basis and have checked the provider's terms. You are responsible for the provider account, its privacy settings, usage limits and any charges. FlowOS does not charge for AI requests; the provider bills the owner of the API key directly. AI output may be inaccurate and must be reviewed.

4. Other service providers and data transfers

FlowOS uses Supabase for account authentication, database storage and backend operations. When enabled, Expo Push Service receives your device push token and a sync marker/timestamp, not task or calendar content. Expo EAS Update receives technical compatibility metadata such as platform and app/runtime version. Google APIs receive information needed for sign-in and the Google synchronization you request. The public website is hosted by Netlify, which may process standard connection metadata such as IP address and browser request information.

When you use voice input, the operating system or browser speech-recognition provider may process audio under its own privacy terms. FlowOS receives the recognized transcript; FlowOS does not upload raw voice recordings to its own backend. Local reminders and diagnostic/notification logs are stored on the device. The current app code does not include advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

5. Purposes and choices

FlowOS processes account and activity data to provide authentication, task/event management, synchronization, reminders, widgets and user support. Optional AI data is processed to provide the specific AI operation you request after your confirmation. You can keep AI disabled, decline an individual confirmation, disconnect Google, disable notifications, or remove your API key at any time.

6. Retention and deletion

FlowOS retains account and application data for as long as needed to provide the service or as required by law. Local diagnostic and notification records remain on the device until cleared by the app or removed with app data. FlowOS does not retain AI prompts or generated responses on its backend; the selected AI provider may retain or use requests according to its own policies and account settings. FlowOS cannot delete data from a provider on your behalf.

You can delete your FlowOS account in the app or use the external account deletion page. Account deletion removes FlowOS account data, stored Google connection data and tokens, commitments and registered push tokens, subject to legal retention requirements. It does not delete data already received by third-party providers or revoke your provider API key. Remove the key separately in FlowOS AI settings and manage/revoke it with the provider.

7. Security

FlowOS uses HTTPS/TLS for network connections, authenticated access controls and row-level security for account data. Google OAuth tokens are held in a private server-side schema. Provider API keys are held in device secure storage and sent only to the selected provider for authentication. No security measure can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights and contact

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Contact support.flowos@gmail.com for privacy requests. You may also disconnect Google, disable AI, remove your API key and control notification permissions in the app/device settings.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when FlowOS features or legal requirements change. The latest version and update date will be published here.
