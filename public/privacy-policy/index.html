FlowOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026
Developer and privacy contact

FlowOS. For privacy questions or requests, contact alex16nardi@gmail.com.

1. What data FlowOS handles

FlowOS handles information needed to provide the service, including your Google account identity (such as email address, profile information and account identifier), FlowOS account identifier, tasks, events, reminders and other commitments that you create, and your application preferences.

2. Google Sign-In and Google services

FlowOS uses Google Sign-In for authentication. The Google authentication flow can provide basic profile information, such as your name, email address and Google account identifier. If you choose to connect Google services, FlowOS accesses the Google Calendar and Google Tasks data covered by the permissions you authorize, in order to synchronize events and tasks with FlowOS.

FlowOS may store the Google account email, Google account identifier, selected calendar and task-list information, synchronization status, and OAuth access/refresh tokens. OAuth tokens are stored server-side in a private database area and are not exposed to the application client.

Google data is used only to provide the user-requested FlowOS features and synchronization. FlowOS does not use Google Calendar or Google Tasks data for advertising or to sell user data.

3. Tasks and commitments

Information you enter into FlowOS, such as titles, descriptions, dates, deadlines, duration, context, priority, status and other commitment information, is stored with your account so that FlowOS can provide its task and planning features.

4. AI features

When you use an AI-powered FlowOS feature, text needed to interpret or plan a commitment may be sent from the FlowOS server to OpenAI for processing. FlowOS uses OpenAI as a service provider for this feature and configures the relevant API requests not to store responses through the API's storage option. Do not enter information into AI features that you do not want processed by an AI service.

5. Location and weather

If you enable the weather feature, FlowOS requests the device's foreground location while the feature is being used to obtain today's weather forecast. Depending on the device permission selected, this may provide precise or approximate coordinates. The coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo solely to request the weather forecast and are not stored by FlowOS as a location history or user profile.

6. Notifications and local diagnostics

FlowOS may use local device notifications to deliver reminders when you enable notifications. FlowOS also keeps limited technical diagnostic and notification-log information locally on your device to help diagnose application problems and reminder scheduling issues. These local records are not transmitted to FlowOS as an analytics or advertising profile.

7. Service providers and data sharing

FlowOS uses third-party infrastructure and services to operate its features, including Supabase for authentication and application data, Google APIs for the Google features you authorize, OpenAI for AI processing when you use AI features, and Open-Meteo for weather forecasts. Data is transmitted to these providers only as necessary to provide the corresponding feature.

8. Security

FlowOS uses authentication controls, encrypted HTTPS connections, row-level access controls for application data, and private server-side storage for Google OAuth tokens. Access to application records is restricted to the authenticated account that owns them.

9. Retention and deletion

FlowOS retains account and application data for as long as necessary to provide the service, unless you request deletion or a longer retention period is required by law. Local diagnostic and notification records remain on the device until they are cleared by the application or removed with the application data.

When an account deletion request is fulfilled, FlowOS deletes the FlowOS account and associated application data, including stored Google connection data and OAuth tokens, subject to any data that must lawfully be retained.

You can initiate deletion from within FlowOS or from the external account deletion page.

10. Your choices

  • You can disconnect Google from FlowOS settings.
  • You can control location and notification permissions through your device settings.
  • You can request deletion of your FlowOS account and associated data.
  • You can contact the privacy address above with questions or privacy requests.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when FlowOS changes or when legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page, with the update date shown at the top.
